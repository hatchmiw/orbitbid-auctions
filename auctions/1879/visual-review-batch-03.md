## Selected independently sourced market references

These corroborate limited individual price contexts, not the entire batch. Avoid treating asking prices as completed sales, and do not extrapolate a like-new price to missing or damaged sets.

- **Lot 18442, BEX13A component:** [M&W Asset Recovery's October 2025 auction](https://www.mnwinc.com/snap-on-bolt-extractor-set-bex13a) records a **$125 winning hammer bid** for an individually boxed, described-complete Snap-On BEX13A extractor kit. It applied an additional 18.5% buyer premium. [KB Tools' now sold listing](https://kbtools.com/product/snap-on-bex13a-4/) carried a **$175 advertised price** for a *like-new* complete example; that posted price is not proof of what the buyer paid. The OrbitBid lot bundles additional extractor kits, some visibly incomplete or worn; do not multiply a standalone complete-kit price indiscriminately.
- **Lot 18441, bushing driver:** A [September 21, 2026 auction](https://hibid.com/arkansas/lot/323004579/snap-on-bushing-driver-set) reports **$150 realized** for a Snap-On bushing driver set, but the listing does not firmly establish that its model is A257. OrbitBid 18441's 28 stamped pieces must be counted; a [retail asking listing for an A257](https://offerup.com/item/detail/2b95fc15-402b-3b81-a2a6-4e62096aa2f6) displays **$500**, which is **not a realized sale**.
- **Lot 18440, RTD33:** A [historical auction result](https://www.liveauctionworld.com/June-25th-Summer-Consignment-Auction_as43196_p2) lists **$60 hammer** for RTD33 thread chasers; current [eBay listings](https://www.ebay.com/p/1023322659) are advertised at roughly **$85–$100 plus shipping**, not verified realized prices. Confirm this case's contents before relying on either reference.
- **Lot 18486, Lang 87:** eBay marks a [March 2026 used example as sold with a $50 listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/406757534799) and a [September 22, 2026 used example with an $80 listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/117423431829). Best-offer acceptance can obscure the actual paid amount. The OrbitBid case appears partly used; verify all small tips before comparing it with complete sets.
