## Important high-risk groups

- **Industrial rigging, wire rope and lifting clamps:** visible corrosion or missing legible certifications; never assume overhead-lifting suitability.
- **Large fuel/oil tanks and cylinders:** unknown ownership, contents, hydrostatic testing, leakage and disposal liabilities.
- **Large truck tires and multi-piece rims:** roadworthiness and ring compatibility require qualified inspection.
- **Vehicles, heavy machinery and attachments:** visual triage only. Mechanical function, titles, loading, haulage, undercarriage wear and operating hours require independent verification.
- **Aged auto-parts cabinets and consumables:** potential value depends on a labor-intensive part-number inventory and demand.
