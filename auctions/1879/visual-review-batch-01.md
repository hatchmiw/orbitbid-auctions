## Market-reference checks

Only selective comparable sales have been checked so far. A [Stihl TS420 offered in a government auction](https://prod-seo.govdeals.com/en/asset/17486/7167) closed in July 2026 at a $410 high bid; its listing says the engine starts but the blade is absent. An [older TS350 in unverified working condition](https://www.purplewave.com/auction/221206/item/MH9890/Stihl-TS350-Concrete_Equipment-Concrete_Equip._or_Acces.-Oklahoma) sold in December 2022 for $143. Those are **different models and conditions** from lot 18138 (exact model unknown and visibly very dusty), so they contextualize rather than establish its resale value. An [OfferUp listing](https://offerup.com/item/detail/a42fd05b-c295-3a9c-b242-590a99172f08) marked sold describes a **restored** Wilton 9-600 at $550 asking/listing price; that is not comparable in condition to the battered auction 18576 vise and is not proof of an actual transaction price.
